# Shared Atlas artwork

These are original production illustration assets generated with the built-in
image-generation tool on 2026-09-25. No reference screenshots were copied into
runtime assets. Text, subjects, links and interaction remain separate UI content.

- `public/art/atlas-island.png`: reusable transparent terrain; visually inspected
  for a clear center, complete perimeter, no text, and clean transparency.
- `public/art/atlas-water.png`: shared illustrated water and distant landscape;
  visually inspected for quiet open water and absence of labels or UI.

## Island prompt

Create a production illustration asset, NOT a UI mockup. A single richly detailed whimsical storybook island, isometric three-quarter view, isolated on a genuinely transparent background. Broad grassy mint-green meadow with empty flat central clearing taking 55% of island surface for a separate UI subject. Rocky layered cliff sides, tiny sandy shoreline, turquoise-white foam hugging the base only, clusters of beautifully drawn evergreen trees and bushes around outer left and right edges, a few pink flowers, warm sunlight, crisp dark-navy hand-inked outlines, soft watercolor/gouache gradients. Charming polished illustrated educational map aesthetic, intricate crafted terrain rather than flat vector shapes. No text, no letters, no labels, no signs, no icons, no buildings, no bridge, no people, no UI. Entire island visible with transparent margins. Wide 4:3 composition. This will be repeated as selectable islands over a separate blue water background in a visual product guide.

## Water prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Generate a wide landscape environment asset for a colorful illustrated product map, no interface. 3:2 landscape composition. Soft luminous turquoise blue tropical sea fills the lower 80% of the image, with subtle hand-painted white curved ripples and delicate aqua variations, mostly calm unobstructed open water for overlaying separate islands and labels in HTML. At very top 20%, distant small hazy blue-green mountain silhouettes and evergreen forested shoreline with tiny clouds in pale blue sky, warm sunlight. Whimsical polished hand-inked storybook gouache watercolor aesthetic, crisp charming outlines and softly textured gradients. A tiny rocky islet at extreme bottom-left edge only. Main central and right water MUST remain empty and visually quiet. No foreground large islands, no buildings, no people, no paths or arrows, no text, no symbols, no labels, no UI, no border. Rich airy bright blues like an illustrated adventure map, not a realistic satellite view. Asset must work as a continuous background beneath separate interactive illustrated islands.

running application makes no font-provider requests. This font license does not
